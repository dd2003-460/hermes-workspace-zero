

### 解答

#### (a) 时间常数 τ = RC  
$$
\tau = R \cdot C = 4.7 \times 10^3 \, \Omega \times 3.3 \times 10^{-9} \, \text{F} = 1.551 \times 10^{-5} \, \text{s}
$$  
**答案：** $ \boxed{1.551 \times 10^{-5} \, \text{s}} $

---

#### (b) 单级截止频率 $ f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi\tau} $  
$$
f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 1.551 \times 10^{-5}} \approx \frac{1}{9.745 \times 10^{-5}} \approx 10261.5 \, \text{Hz}
