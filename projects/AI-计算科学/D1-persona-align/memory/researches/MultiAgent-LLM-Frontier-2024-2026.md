## 3. Cho et al. (2025) — Herd Behavior in Multi-Agent LLMs

**Authors**: Young-Min Cho, Sharath Chandra Guntuku, Lyle Ungar
**arXiv**: 2505.21588
**Date**: May 2025

**Summary**:
LLMs modify their outputs when exposed to peer suggestions. Confidence mismatches trigger conformity patterns. Response formatting alters interaction strength.

**Relevance**: MEDIUM-HIGH
