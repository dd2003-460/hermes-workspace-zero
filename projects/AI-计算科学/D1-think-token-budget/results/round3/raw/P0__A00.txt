Problem: Find the sum of all integer bases b>9 for which 17_b divides 97_b.

Solution: 17_b = b+7, 97_b = 9b+7. Since 9b+7 = 9(b+7) − 56, we need (b+7) | 56. With b>9, b+7 > 16; divisors of 56 exceeding 16 are 28 and 56, giving b = 21 and b = 49.
