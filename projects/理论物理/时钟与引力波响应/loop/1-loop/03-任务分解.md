    Args:
        tG, pG: 2D GW direction grids (ntheta, nphi).
        satellite_positions: (3, 3) array of satellite positions.
        L: arm length (m).
        det_type: 'olc' (default) or 'interferometer'.

    Returns:
        (R_plus, R_cross) — complex arrays.
    """

        normal: (3,) normal vector of the triangle.

    Returns:
        (R_plus, R_cross) — complex arrays.
