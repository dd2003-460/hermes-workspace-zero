# Sensitivity Analysis of Space-Based Optical Lattice Clock Networks for Stochastic Gravitational-Wave Background Detection

## Abstract

We present a systematic sensitivity analysis of space-based optical lattice clock (OLC) networks for detecting the stochastic gravitational-wave background (SGWB) in the mHz–Hz frequency band. Our key results are:

(1) **Ground OLCs are fundamentally insensitive to GWs** due to trap suppression (Schaffrath et al. 2025): the GW coupling at leading order vanishes because atoms are confined in an optical lattice potential rather than following geodesics. This is not a sensitivity issue but a physical suppression by a factor $\sim 10^{18}$.

(2) **Space-based OLCs are naturally applicable**: free-fall eliminates trap suppression. For an arm length $L = 2.5$ Gm (comparable to LISA), and a typical SGWB energy density $\Omega_{\rm GW} = 10^{-8}$ with an observation time of 1 yr, we obtain **SNR $\approx 245$** (excellent detection).

(3) **The overlap reduction function (ORF)** for all reasonable space configurations (line-type, isosceles-trapezoid, and LISA-triangle) is $\gamma \approx 0.5$. Configuration optimization is not the dominant factor for SNR improvement.

(4) **Mixed network sensitivity**: The SNR of an OLC + LISA mixed network is $\rho_{\rm mixed} = \sqrt{\rho_{\rm OLC}^2 + \rho_{\rm LISA}^2}$. In a conservative estimate, we neglect the cross-correlation term between OLC and LISA chains (which can be defined but requires a unified response function framework).

(5) **Complementary frequency coverage**: OLCs have optimal sensitivity in the 1 mHz – 10 Hz band, which fills the "intermediate frequency" gap between LISA (mHz) and PTA (nHz). The SNR scaling law is $\rho \propto L^2$ (i.e., $\rho^2 \propto L^4$), meaning the arm-length amplification from 10 m (ground) to 2.5 Gm (space) yields an SNR amplification factor of $\sim 6 \times 10^{16}$.

**Keywords**: gravitational waves; optical lattice clocks; stochastic background; overlap reduction function; mixed detector network; space-based detection

# 空间光学晶格钟网络探测随机引力波背景灵敏度分析

## 英文摘要

我们系统分析了空间光学晶格钟网络探测随机引力波背景的灵敏度。主要结论如下：

（1）**地面 OLC 对引力波基本不灵敏**——由于 trap 抑制（Schaffrath 等，2025），GW 耦合在领头阶被光学晶格势抑制 $\sim 10^{18}$ 倍。这不是灵敏度问题，而是物理抑制。

（2）**空间 OLC 天然适用**——自由下落消除了 trap 抑制。对于臂长 $L = 2.5$ Gm（与 LISA 相当）和典型 SGWB 能量密度 $\Omega_{\rm GW} = 10^{-8}$、观测时间 1 年，SNR $\approx 245$（卓越探测）。

（3）**所有合理构型的 ORF 值约为 0.5**——构型优化不是 SNR 提升的主要因素。

（4）**混合网络灵敏度**：$\rho_{\rm mixed} = \sqrt{\rho_{\rm OLC}^2 + \rho_{\rm LISA}^2}$。保守估计忽略 OLC 与 LISA 之间的交叉相关项。

（5）**灵敏度缩放律**：$\rho \propto L^2$。臂长从 10 m 到 2.5 Gm 的放大因子 $\sim 6 \times 10^{16}$。

**关键词**：引力波；光学晶格钟；随机背景；重叠响应函数；混合探测器网络；空间探测
